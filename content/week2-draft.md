## Day 8 · Fri 10/16 · QUEEN · Real Talk — "Her Side of the Bed"
Casting: Black woman, early 30s · Image ready: `gen_cheating_doorway.png`

1. She came home early because she wanted to surprise him.
2. His truck was in the driveway. That was the first thing that felt off.
3. Bedroom door was cracked. She heard laughing. Not his.
4. She pushed the door open. Somebody was on her side of the bed.
5. He said, "It's not what it looks like." It was exactly what it looked like.
6. She didn't scream. She just picked up her keys and walked back out.
7. From the book ✅ Queen Day 62: "When have you known something was wrong but talked yourself out of it?"
8. Question: Be honest. Were the signs there before that day?
9. CTA: LEVEL UP, QUEEN — For the woman who stopped talking herself out of what she knows.

**Caption:** She didn't scream. She didn't throw nothing. She just left. Would you have stayed calm? #LevelUpQueen #RealTalk #Relationships #Trust

## Day 9 · Sat 10/17 · KING · Real Talk — "The Corner"
Casting: Latino father and his 13-year-old son · neighborhood at dusk

2. Thirteen. Hoodie up. Trying to look older than he is.
3. He pulled up and said, "Get in the car."
4. His son said, "Why? You did the same thing."
5. And he didn't have an answer for that.
6. Because he did. And he knows exactly where that road goes.
7. From the book ✅ King Day 42: "What pattern from your family are you living out automatically, without consciously choosing it?"
8. Question: How do you tell your son not to do what you did?
9. CTA: LEVEL UP, KING — 100 Days of Faith, Discipline, Purpose, and a Better You.

**Caption:** "You did the same thing." What would you say back? Fathers, I need to hear from you. #LevelUpKing #Fatherhood #RealTalk #BreakTheCycle

## Day 10 · Sun 10/18 · BUNDLE · Who's Right? — "The $300 to His Mother"
Casting: Black couple, late 20s · kitchen table, bills spread out

1. Hook: Every month he sends his mother $300. This month the light bill is past due.
2. HER: "We got a shutoff notice and you still sent your mama money?"
3. HIM: "She raised me by herself. I'm not about to let her go without."
4. HER: "I'm not asking you to cut her off. I'm asking you to put us first."
5. HIM: "So I gotta choose between you and my mother now?"
6. She's tired of the lights being a maybe. He's not leaving his mother hanging.
7. Question: Is he a good son or a bad partner? Can he be both?
8. CTA: LEVEL UP, KING + LEVEL UP, QUEEN. Two perspectives. Real-life struggles.

**Caption:** She has bills. He has a mother who did everything for him. Who's right? KING or QUEEN 👇🏾 #WhosRight #LevelUpKing #LevelUpQueen #RelationshipTalk

7. From the book ✅ Queen Day 56: "What shame are you carrying that belongs to someone else or to a system?"

## Day 12 · Tue 10/20 · KING · Text Thread — "Unknown Number"
Casting: Pacific Islander man, 30s · phone screen, late night

1. Hook: Fifteen years. No calls. No birthdays. Then this.
2. Thread:
   - Unknown: "Is this Andre?"
   - Him: "Who is this"
   - Unknown: "It's your father. I know I don't deserve this."
   - Unknown: "I'm sick. I just want to see you."
3. He read it four times.
4. He typed "Too late." Deleted it.
5. He typed "Ok." Deleted that too.
6. From the book ✅ King Day 41: "How has your father's absence shaped your beliefs about belonging, worth, and commitment?"
7. Question: Do you answer? Or let it sit like he let you sit?
8. CTA: LEVEL UP, KING — For the man still carrying what his father left him.

**Caption:** Fifteen years of nothing, then "I'm sick." What you texting back? #LevelUpKing #Fatherhood #RealTalk #Healing

## Day 14 · Thu 10/22 · BUNDLE · One Page That Hit Different — "The Door"
Casting: Black father, 30s, and his 7-year-old daughter · narrow apartment hallway

1. Hook: Bad day at work. Boss on him all shift. Car making that noise again.
2. He came home and slammed the door so hard the picture fell.

4. Then she got real quiet. Like she'd learned to.
5. He's not a bad man. But that look on her face stayed with him all night.
6. From the book ✅ King Day 28: "How does your anger affect the people around you, and who bears the most damage?"
7. You can be stressed and still choose what your kids remember.
8. CTA: LEVEL UP, KING + LEVEL UP, QUEEN. Faith for real life.

**Caption:** It wasn't about her. But she's the one who flinched. Who else grew up learning to get quiet when the door slammed? #LevelUpKing #LevelUpQueen #Fatherhood #RealTalk
