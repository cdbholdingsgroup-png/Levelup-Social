# Level Up voice guide

Words are set by Eric. Any draft is a proposal for his review, never final copy.

1. Tell what happened. Don't explain a lesson.
2. More dialogue, less narration. Let us hear what someone actually said.
3. Specific moments, not categories: the knock on the door, the "K" text, $400 short the day before rent.
4. No generic motivational lines ("small steps, done daily...").
5. Don't resolve every argument. End on a question that makes people pick a side.
6. Faith shows up naturally beside real needs ("You can pray and still need help"). Never a lecture.
7. "Sis" / "Bro" only when natural, not in every post.
8. Short slides. One line of dialogue per slide.
9. Book quotes are word for word from the manuscripts, labeled with the day.
